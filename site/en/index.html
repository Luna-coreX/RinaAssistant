What happens between the phrase and the action

Five steps, and at any of them Rina may stop and ask. None of them leaves your machine.

01 Heard The segmenter cuts the phrase away from the silence and recognition turns it into text. Vosk and Whisper work offline.
02 Parsed Parsing looks for the intent and its arguments: which program, how many minutes, what to say. What it does not recognise goes to a web search or to the local model.
03 Asked Several matches, an unsigned program, anything irreversible: each one produces a question describing what will happen.
04 Done The tool is called through the core's single registry, where its permissions are checked. The machine is touched by the shell.
05 Recorded The journal takes the call: which tool, which arguments, what came of it. Free text is replaced by its length.
The irreversible is marked with hatching — the same hatching the program uses. There is no red in the palette at all.

What she does

Works by voice from the first launch, with nothing to set up. The interface has five languages; she is spoken to in Russian.

You can try the window here: the mock-up answers with the same recorded phrases that pin her parsing down. It is in Russian, as the commands are.

She finds your programs herself

The index is built from Start menu shortcuts, the registry, Store apps and any portable folders you point her at.

“Открой телеграм” finds Telegram: Cyrillic is matched loosely against Latin. Your answer to a clarifying question becomes an alias.

A command is a graph

Steps are nodes with wires between them; a simple command is a graph of one node. Six kinds of action and seven kinds of step: pause, repeat, condition, stop, calling another command, remembering a value.

Anything brought from another machine arrives switched off.

Voice both ways

The answer is synthesised sentence by sentence and starts sounding before it is finished. A new phrase cuts the previous one off, so you can interrupt with a word.

Speech: silent, pyttsx3, Edge Neural TTS, gTTS, Piper. Hearing: off, Vosk, Whisper — an engine that opens the microphone itself is not offered here, because the sound arrives from the shell.

Reminders and sixteen system actions

“Поставь таймер на десять минут”, “напомни через полчаса позвонить маме”. They fire from a background scheduler, with the window closed and from the tray.

Volume, skip and pause, lock, screenshot, sleep, restart, shut down — and the ones that move her own window.

Plugins, each in its own process

A plugin declares tools, settings and a section; the core checks its permissions, the same ones the built-in tools get.

Six ship with her: Clock, Dice, Greeter, Notes, Convert and Rates. Notes has a section of its own, Convert and Rates put a tile on the home screen, and the rest answer by voice. A seventh rides along as a sample for people writing plugins; how to write your own is on the developer page.

Three finishes, five languages

Silver, black and graphite, with a choice of accent. Русский, English, Українська, Español, Deutsch. A floating command line over your windows, a tray icon, autostart, hotkeys.

Colour, size and motion are set by values in the design system, and the window is compared against them pixel by pixel.

Nothing leaves until you ask for it

There is no account. There is no telemetry. There is nothing to collect.

Never leaves

  • The recorded audio and the recognised text: Vosk and Whisper do their counting on your machine.
  • The contents of your files, notes and conversation history.
  • Paths on your disk, program names, tokens and passwords.
  • The fact that you launched something at all.

A diagnostic package is collected only when you ask for it, is anonymised, and says inside what it left out.

Leaves when you ask for it

  • Edge Neural TTS and gTTS: the phrase to be spoken.
  • Web search for an unrecognised phrase: the phrase itself; asking for music opens a YouTube search.
  • The local model: the question goes to the address in settings, by default http://localhost:11434.
  • Downloading models: whichever you picked in the wizard — Vosk from alphacephei.com, Whisper and Piper from huggingface.co.
  • Checking for updates: a request to api.github.com when you press “Check”.
  • The Rates plugin: a request to the central bank's website; the weather sample, if you switch it on, to open-meteo.com.

You are the one who switches each of these on, and the settings label it — including a warning when the model's address turns out not to be local.

“What Rina knows about me” A page inside the program: learned words, conversation history, reminders, to-dos, your own commands, command statistics, plugins, program search folders and changed settings — and beside them a button to forget it. All of it, or one at a time.

Five minutes and no questions about privileges

Rina installs into your user profile. She needs no access to system folders.

A ready installer

There has been no release yet. The beta is being built; as soon as the installer is out, the link will appear here and on the releases page.

It does not touch PATH or register the Python it brings, does not ask for administrator rights, installs no service and switches on no autostart: autostart is a setting inside the program.

From source, right now

You need Windows, Python 3.10 or newer, and .NET 9.

git clone https://github.com/Luna-coreX/RinaAssistant.git
cd RinaAssistant
pip install -r requirements.txt
dotnet run --project shell/Rina.Shell

The shell starts the core itself. On the first launch a short wizard asks which voice and which recognition you want, and downloads only those.

Then say it — or type it: “открой блокнот”, “поставь таймер на 5 минут”, “сколько будет 17 на 23”.

What is free and what may cost money later

Written down now, while there is nothing paid.

The beta is free All of it. There is no payment code in the program at all.
The local base stays free Voice, launching programs, commands, reminders, plugins, the local model, the privacy page. All of it is under Apache-2.0, and a copy you have cannot have its licence taken back.
What may cost money later is what gets added Most likely the things that cost money to run: a service on a server, a connector to a paid third-party service.
What stays open Handling sound and your data, launching programs safely, confirming the dangerous, the security journal, the protocol and the base functionality.
